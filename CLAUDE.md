# Claude entry point

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the project layout, engineering coaching, installed CLI and
MCP repair loop. Complete the [pinned tooling setup](README.md#first-run-setup) first.
The [MCP guide](docs/workflow/MCP.md) explains client setup, bounded paths and capability flags.
The [diagnostic guide](docs/workflow/DIAGNOSTICS.md) explains short and full output,
per-run logs and failed checks. Keep electrical requirements, mechanical review and human
approval explicit; do not turn an observed model or successful command into a design approval.
